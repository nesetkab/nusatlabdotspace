<Section id="teams">
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Our teams" title="How the work splits up" />

<Grid cols={4}>
  <Card icon="wrench" title="Hardware">
    Mechanical and electrical engineering for spacecraft and ground hardware. Mechanical members CAD in SolidWorks and
    Onshape, build with 3D printers, laser cutters, and CNC in EXP and the AerospaceNU back lab, and run structural,
    thermal, and frequency analyses. Electrical and embedded members design PCBs and dev boards, test and validate
    space hardware, and write firmware.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="zap" title="Software">
    Flight and ground-station software: sensing, control, imaging, and compression algorithms, plus satellite
    communication, networking, and scheduling systems, tested and merged across every mission subsystem. This
    semester's goal is a working FlatSat by January.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="globe" title="Simulations">
    Orbital and mission simulation with tools like FreeFlyer and Systems Tool Kit, scientific computing to verify
    mission and system architecture, and Python or MATLAB/Simulink models to test algorithms, kinematics, and control
    loops.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="briefcase" title="Business">
    Finding and winning competitions and proposals from NASA, AFRL, IEEE, and more; reaching out to researchers and
    institutions; logistics, operations, and finance; merch and branding; and recruitment campaigns and community
    engagement.
  </Card>
</Grid>
</Section>
