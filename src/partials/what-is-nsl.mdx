NSL started in 2020 as Project Horizon, a satellite team within AeroNU, Northeastern's aerospace club, and became its
own club in Spring 2026. The club is run by a management team of 10 undergrads and supported by our faculty advisor, Dr. Josep
Jornet, the Associate Dean of Research in the Northeastern College of Engineering. We're also backed by a small but
dedicated group of satellite club alumni.
